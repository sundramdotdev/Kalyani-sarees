# Local SEO & GEO Strategy — Kalyani Sarees Dhanpatganj

## 1. Local Strategic Context

The business combines a high-fashion digital storefront with a rooted physical retail showroom in **Dhanpatganj, Sultanpur District, Uttar Pradesh**. 

Local shoppers across Dhanpatganj, Sultanpur, Ayodhya (Faizabad), Amethi, and Pratapgarh seek authentic wedding sarees and festive clothing. The GEO strategy ensures that when regional customers search for ethnic fashion, **Kalyani Sarees Dhanpatganj** ranks in the **Google Local 3-Pack**, **Google Maps**, and localized organic web search results.
