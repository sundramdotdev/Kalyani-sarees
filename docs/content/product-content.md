## 4. Textile Care Instructions

Because pure silks and handcrafted zari are heirloom garments, every product page includes standardized wash and care guidance:
1. **Dry Clean Only**: Always recommend professional dry cleaning for pure silk, Banarasi, and zari embroidered items.
2. **Storage**: Fold carefully and wrap in clean, breathable unbleached muslin cotton cloth. Never hang heavy silk sarees on wire hangers to avoid yarn stretching.
3. **Ironing**: Steam press or iron on low silk setting with a protective cotton pressing cloth between the iron and the zari threads.
4. **Moisture & Fragrance**: Avoid spraying perfume, deodorants, or water directly onto gold and silver zari to prevent oxidation and discoloration.
